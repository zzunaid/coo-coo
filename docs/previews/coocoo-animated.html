What's animating

When idle, Gerald has a slow continuous breathing motion. His belly expands and contracts. Every 4–6 seconds he blinks. Every so often his eyes dart left or right, like he's actually watching something. Subtle but it makes him feel aware.

  • belly-breathe — body ellipse ry oscillates by ±2px on 3s loop
  • blink — both eyes scale to 0 vertically every 4.5s
  • pupil-dart — pupils translate ±2px to glance around
  • head-tilt — head rotates ±4° on a long lazy cycle

Animation layers (how it's built)

1. Continuous (always on)
Breathing belly, blinking, pupil dart. Makes Gerald feel alive in any state.
2. Idle-only
Slow head tilt left/right. Gentle vertical bob.
3. State motion
Shake (panic), peck (thinking), zzz drift (sleepy), happy bob (done).
4. Transitions
Wings spread out instantly on panic. Eyes shift smoothly between expressions.
